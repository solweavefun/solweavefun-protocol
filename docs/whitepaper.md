# SOLWEAVE Whitepaper

## Vision and mission

Markets for on-chain trading cards are noisy: thousands of tokens, dozens of dashboards, no shared picture. **Vision:** one fabric where every thread of activity is visible and honest. **Mission:** weave public Solana data into a fast, calm, verifiable board that anyone can read in seconds, and keep it free of hidden claims.

## Principles

1. Public data only, always attributable to its source.
2. When a feed fails, show the last good numbers and a dash, never a guess.
3. Every external fact (address, links, endpoints) lives in one record with an explicit state.
4. Nothing is presented as advice.

- **Phase 1: Warp.** Launch the live market board, ticker and brand site.
- **Phase 2: Weft.** Pin the own-token row once the address is public; add deeper per-token detail.
- **Phase 3: Selvedge.** Harden data handling: more sources, cross-checks, clearer outage states.
- **Phase 4: Pattern.** Community tools and open documentation.
